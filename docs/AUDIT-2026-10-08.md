### What that costs him

1. **One change doesn't reach everything.** Change `.btn` and 130 buttons keep their own size/colour/fade, and ~145 click targets aren't buttons at all.
2. **Keyboard and screen readers miss most of the app.** 439 clickable spans/divs, **0 with tabIndex**, 5 with a role. 33 aria-labels in the whole renderer. Tab skips them; Enter does nothing.
3. **Fake disabled.** 38 buttons fade with opacity but stay clickable; a real disabled state needs a real button.
4. **Themes leak.** 51 hard-coded colours don't follow a theme change (Timeline is the worst).
5. **Layer fights.** 22 z-index values; menus, dialogs, toasts and drag ghosts can stack in the wrong order (ch.01 already calls this a wart class).
6. **Icon strays.** ~80 glyphs carry meanings the ch.03 vocabulary never registered (law: "new meanings enter this table before they enter an app"), and 14 ♪ survive the drawn score icon.

**Known since July.** KOAN.design ch.09 (the vi-dancer gap checklist, 2026-07-29) already lists most of this: one context menu, one input recipe, one ✕ close idiom, one legend recipe, bare `.lb`, type-scale migration, keyboard-focusable buttons, `--warn`/`--focus`, accent var rename, tokens adoption, one z-index module, icon library migration. None is ticked. Two of its items (radius 5 → 4, mono chrome) were superseded by his 6 Oct call (ch.14: Video Dancer's sans controls and 5px buttons are the reference).

## Part 3. Workflows, end to end

Sources: `docs/WORKFLOW.md` (the laws), `docs/workflow-audit-2026-07-18.md` (placement law), the public AI-MANUAL (0.26.0), the panel registry (`App.tsx` PANEL_DEFS), the code paths named below, Astra's 05-WORKFLOWS as leads, and the comparison apps listed at the end.

**Correction (10-08, his catch):** the first draft of this part treated takes as versions of one shot to compare and pick a winner. That is wrong. It came from `docs/WORKFLOW.md` ("Keeper ★ = current best"), which predates keepers becoming flags (list 6: "a keeper — a flag, many per set", `takeRows.tsx`). WORKFLOW.md still teaches the old model; that is a doc fix for his list.

**The working model.** Sources (Clip Gen, Image Gen, Score, Voice, SFX, Sheet Gen, the two edit trees) produce **libraries of takes**. Variation is deliberate: he changes settings between takes, or batches 20 variations, to have many different takes to work with. Takes are the material. He marks them (★ flag, colour, disabled, ~ tag) and pulls individual takes out into use: onto the timeline as pinned blocks, into the Bin as new clips, into the Library, into ref slots, onto fields to restore a recipe, into the editors, into the next generation. There is no one right take; selecting one never retires the others. The ★ flagged last is the one dynamic blocks play.

The loop: **make variety → mark it → pull takes out into use → feed them back into the next generation.** Every stage can bounce back to the plan.
