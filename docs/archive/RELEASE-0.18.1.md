## Video Dancer 0.18.1

- **Dissolves you can drag.** Drag the small cut marker to create one, drag either edge for length, double-click for exact duration and Center, Start or End at cut. Stripes tell you when source footage runs out and edge frames repeat.
- **Moving shots through the blend.** Preview and export share the overlap, retain effects and use the available source handles. Frame grabs capture the blended picture.
- **Select together, delete together.** Right-click a selection across Bin and Library to act on all of it. One Undo restores a bulk deletion; source files stay on disk.
- **Watch the cut.** Review the whole timeline or your marked range, with motion, effects and sound. Ask in the Timeline toolbar or through the Co-Director. Watch has a model setting; Google failures can fall back to clearly labelled still-frame review.
- **Voice names its supplier.** VIA shows who runs the model and whose credits it spends. Image and clone-sample uploads keep their file type; access errors name the failed step.
- **Takes become references.** Drag an Image Gen take straight into reference A or B, including takes from another card.
- **Steadier editing.** The monitor keeps its picture while scrubbing. Save failures report properly, recovered renders avoid duplicate takes, imports stay with their original project, and dialogs keep keyboard focus.

Windows updates automatically. On macOS, download the new installer above.
