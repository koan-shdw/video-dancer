## Video Dancer 0.20.1

- **Every project, one window.** Open lists every project you have opened, no more limit of eight. Four across, four rows, scroll for the rest, drag the strip under the cards to make the list taller. Double-click opens.
- **Folders for projects.** Right-click a card for new folder, drag cards onto folders, select several and file them together. Folders live in the window only; nothing on disk moves.
- **Lost projects come back.** On first launch the app looks beside the projects it knows and adds what it finds. Find projects picks a folder and adds everything inside.
- **Open in Photoshop.** Right-click a Library image, an Image Gen take or an Image Edit result and choose open in. The app opens a copy, watches for your save, PSD included, and asks to import it when you come back. The original never changes.
- **Image Edit picks its model.** Nano Banana, Nano Banana 2, Nano Banana Pro, GPT Image 2 and Seedream 5.0 Pro, with VIA, size and quality beside it. Each result remembers what made it.
- **Select where the edit happens.** Box or lasso an area. The model still sees the whole picture; only your selection changes and the rest stays your original pixels.
- **Compare.** The base image next to the result, side by side, in Image Edit.
- **The results tree zooms.** Slider on the tree, or ctrl and scroll.
- **Workspaces, front and center.** The switcher moved to the middle of the top bar, bigger and bolder.
- **Saves survive cloud sync.** A project inside Dropbox or OneDrive keeps saving while the sync client holds the file.
- **Image Gen fails fast and says why.** An engine crash stops the wait in seconds, oversized references shrink before they load, and running out of memory is named in plain words.
- **Moods appear at once.** A mood the Co-Director saves shows in the MOODS fold right away.

Windows updates automatically. On macOS, download the new installer above.
